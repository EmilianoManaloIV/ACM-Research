# Research

The question-level record, above individual experiments. The workflow and gates are described in [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).

- `questions/RQ-NNN-short-name.md`: one per research question, from [`questions/_template.md`](questions/_template.md). Its gate log is the record of what the research director approved.
- `evidence/RQ-NNN.md`: the literature search and evidence table for that question, from [`evidence/_template.md`](evidence/_template.md).
- [`decisions.md`](decisions.md): research and architecture decisions, newest first.
