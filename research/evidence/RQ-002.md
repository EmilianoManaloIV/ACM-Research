## Disagreements between sources

- **What "better weights" means.** Learned methods are scored mostly by agreement with artist weights (L1, precision, recall). The cut-cell preprint reports that gains look different under a deformation error. Artist weights are not ground truth, and the director has already decided they are a separate reference measure, so RQ-002 should lead with deformation metrics.
- **BBW's quality and robustness.** BBW is widely cited as high quality, while SkinCells and the cut-cell preprint report that its volumetric meshing fails or is costly on production meshes. Creature assets are exactly such meshes.
- **Geodesic voxel binding's competitiveness.** RigNet reports beating it clearly. A search summary of Puppeteer's table puts it close to RigNet on Articulation-XL2.0 (L1 0.745 against 0.729). Both claims are unverified here.

## Open gaps and uncertainties

1. **No independent comparison of the geometric baselines** (bone heat, BBW, voxel binding) on non-human body plans was found. That is the gap RQ-002 can fill first.
2. **Deformation quality is rarely the headline metric** in the learned-skinning literature, which supports RQ-002's metric choice.
3. **Data access.** The most promising dataset needs Hugging Face, which is blocked here, and a per-object licence filter.
4. **Implementations.** Bone heat is available through Blender, BBW through libigl. Geodesic voxel binding has no known open implementation. Learned baselines need GPU inference and their weights, which are on Hugging Face.
5. **Full texts unread.** Every number quoted above needs checking against the paper before it appears in a protocol or result.
